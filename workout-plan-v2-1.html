Protein guide

Aim for 1.6 to 2 g per kg a day, split over 4 to 5 meals of 25 to 40 g each. Protein on the label is the cooked or dry weight shown below.

Food swaps

About 30 g protein
  • 100 g chicken breast
  • 140 g fish
  • 170 g paneer
  • 60 g dry soya chunks
  • 5 whole eggs
  • 1 scoop whey (24 g) plus 1 egg
About 50 g carbs
  • 180 g cooked rice
  • 3 rotis
  • 75 g dry oats
  • 250 g sweet potato
  • 2 bananas and 1 roti
About 10 g fat
  • 2 tsp oil or ghee
  • 20 g peanuts
  • 20 g almonds
  • 1 whole egg and a handful of nuts
Budget protein
  • Eggs, soya chunks, dal with rice, curd, chickpeas, peanuts
  • No whey? Use 4 egg whites, 100 g paneer or 40 g soya in that meal.

Carb and fat tips

  • Put about 30% of carbs 1 to 2 hours before training and 30% after it.
  • Pick rice, roti, oats, sweet potato and fruit. Keep sweets and juice small.
  • Fibre: 25 to 30 g a day from veg, dal and fruit.
  • Keep fat near 25% of calories. Cook with 1 to 2 tsp oil a meal.
  • On rest days, trim carbs by one fist of rice or roti and keep protein the same.

Training and recovery

  • 3 to 4 sets of 8 to 12 reps. Rest 60 to 90 seconds.
  • Weak muscle first. Add a little weight or a rep each week.
  • Sleep 7 to 8 hours. Sunday is rest.
  • Judge weight by the weekly trend, not one day.
